A new project starts with the line's umbrella mark - a lambda on graphite - so
that its icons, favicon and installers are never the framework's default. That
placeholder is meant to be replaced: a product that ships it looks like every
other project that never got a mark of its own.

lyrn records the fact in lyrn.toml when it generates the project, rather than
leaving the doctor to compare icon bytes against a copy of the placeholder,
which would go stale the first time the placeholder is redrawn.

Choose the product's mark, put its masters in assets/, and set
`mark = "chosen"` under [project] in lyrn.toml. This only warns: the mark is a
matter of taste, not of correctness.
