The release page is what a stranger downloads from, and what the installers
and an npm wrapper fetch: a tag whose page is missing, still a draft, or
short of an archive installs nothing on the machines it was built for.

With --release the check asks the forge for the page of the release and
holds it to `[page] assets` in release.toml, with {tag} and {version} filled
in. The question is furca's own: `furca release verify` asks it the same way.
